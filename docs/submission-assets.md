# Submission assets

What we hand the judges, in addition to the live demo.

## Source

The repo at `Team-Chappal/Shruti` is the primary deliverable. The
git history is part of the proof of work; the rebuild recipe in
`tools/rebuild/recipe.md` walks the judges through re-creating the
build on a fresh laptop.

## Architecture diagram

The diagram from Part 2 of `SHRUTI_BATTLE_PLAN.md` (the laptop /
master phone / two element phones layout with the signal chain
annotated) is the canonical architecture picture. The figure is
generated by hand for the printed submission; `tools/diagram.py`
scaffolds a programmatic regeneration if needed.

## Sync-stats snapshot

The most convincing single number is the live "42 microseconds"
reading on the sync-stats overlay during the demo. A screenshot of
that overlay, with the 5-hour uptime stamp visible, goes into the
submission pack as `assets/sync-stats-demo.png`.

## Tape

A 90-second video of the toggle moment — RAW to BEAMFORMED — with
the 42-microsecond reading visible. Recorded on the laptop with
`ffmpeg -f gdigrab` (Windows) or `ffmpeg -f x11grab` (Linux). The
video file lives in `assets/toggle-moment.mp4` and is too large for
the repo (keep the first/last 10 seconds in-repo, full video in
the release tarball).

## Pitch script

`tools/rebuild/demo-runbook.md` is the canonical 4-minute pitch
script, written to be delivered word-for-word. The team rehearses
it three times on event eve; freeze changes after.

## What we do NOT include

- Hard claims about clock precision that we have not measured on
  the actual loaner fleet. The battle plan is the design; the
  recorded sync-stats is the proof.
- Marketing-style claims ("best in class", "industry-leading").
  The pitch stays technical; the jury decides what is best.
- The recorded real-room corpus, if it includes third-party
  voices. Strip or replace with consent.
